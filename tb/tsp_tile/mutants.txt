# tb/tsp_tile/mutants.txt: the planted bugs of M11's shade, fog, SupSample, blend and feedback stages and the pipe in
# rtl/v1/tsp (PLAN M11 "Mutants", conventions.md 7.7), run by tools/gate/mutants.py (make tsp-tile-mutants). Every one
# must be caught by its probe (or, written !<probe>, must pass it: a timing mechanism the values may not see) and the
# unchanged folder must pass first. The texture units' are tb/tsp_tile/mutants_tex.txt, the tile buffers'
# mutants_tbuf.txt.
#
# The probes: `shade` the unit section P7 - P10 on 40,000 random pixels; the tile probes the TSP tile testbench on a
# capture's first renders (tb/tsp_tile/mutant_cases.txt lists them for the captures): blend (7,200 pre-sorted passes a
# render), blendsel2 (the stuck alpha), offspersist (offsKept in the TSP's order), ptruns (run-only pixels in the
# punch-through passes), `presort` scenegen seed 180 (a pre-sorted primitive whose first pixel is the one before's last:
# the only capture of the tile subset and M11's pages where the P10 forward shows, agents/m11 mutscan), `ptmv` seed 22
# (PT_MV's opaque pixels with an alpha under PT_ALPHA_REF; none of the console pages has one).
#
# Format: <id> | <file in rtl/v1/tsp> | <original fragment> | <mutated fragment> | <probe> [args] | <what it breaks>
# (\n a newline, \| a bar, \\ a backslash in a fragment).
@dir rtl/v1/tsp TSP_DIR
@obj TSP_TILE_OBJDIR
@probe shade tsp-unit-build unit/tb --only shade --n 40000 --max-fails 1
@probe blend tsp-tile-build tb --vramvary 7 --renders 0-1 --max-fails 1 build/pre/blend
@probe blendsel2 tsp-tile-build tb --vramvary 7 --renders 0-7 --max-fails 1 build/pre/blendsel2
@probe offspersist tsp-tile-build tb --vramvary 7 --renders 0-7 --max-fails 1 build/pre/offspersist
@probe ptruns tsp-tile-build tb --vramvary 7 --renders 0-7 --max-fails 1 build/pre/ptruns
@probe presort tsp-tile-build tb --vramvary 7 --renders 0-7 --max-fails 1 build/pre/gen_180
@probe ptmv tsp-tile-build tb --vramvary 7 --renders 0-7 --max-fails 1 build/pre/gen_22
